---
name: Handover
description: Write a handover brief so a fresh session can continue the work
placement: append
order: 60
---
This session is near its context limit. Write a handover brief so a fresh session can pick up the work with zero prior context. Include:

- **Goal:** what we are trying to achieve, and why.
- **Done:** what was completed and verified, with file paths and commits.
- **In progress:** what is half-finished, and its exact current state.
- **Next:** remaining steps, in order, with enough detail to act on.
- **Decisions:** choices made along the way and the reasoning, so they are not relitigated.
- **Gotchas:** dead ends already tried, traps, and anything non-obvious the next session must know.
- **References:** files, docs, URLs, and commands needed to keep going.

Be specific: exact paths, names, and values, not summaries. Do not include filler or history that no longer matters. Output only the brief, ready to paste into a new session.
